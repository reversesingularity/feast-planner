Admin Guide — Feast Registration 2026

For Pastor Jon Pinelli  ·  Confidential

1. The Website Address

Open any web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari) and go to:

https://feast-planner.vercel.app/auth/signin

You can bookmark this page so you don't have to type it each time.

2. Creating Your Account (First Time Only)

You'll need to create an account the first time. This only takes a couple of minutes.

1
Go to the website address above. You'll see the Sign In page.
2
Click the grey "Create Account" button at the bottom of the page.
3
Enter your name, email address (use jon.pinelli@cogwa.org), and a password of your choice. Then click "Create Account".
4
Check your email inbox — you'll receive a 6-digit verification code. Type it into the box on screen and click "Verify Email".
5
You're now signed in. After verification, go to the website address above and sign in normally.
⚠️ Important — use the right email
Your admin access is tied specifically to jon.pinelli@cogwa.org. Please use exactly that email when creating your account. If you use a different email address, you won't be able to access the Admin Dashboard.

3. Signing In (Every Other Time)

1
Go to the website address above.
2
Enter your email and password, then click "Sign In".
3
You'll be taken to your personal registration page. From there, click "Admin" in the top menu bar to open the Admin Dashboard.
💡 Forgot your password?
Click "Forgot password?" on the Sign In page. Enter your email address and you'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

4. Using the Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard has four tabs along the top. Click each tab to switch between them.

Tab What it shows
📊 Overview At-a-glance counts — total households, how many have submitted, confirmed, or are still incomplete. Also shows total headcount of adults and children.
📋 Registrations Full list of every registration. Search by name, email, or city. Filter by status (Submitted, Confirmed, Draft, Cancelled).
🤝 Volunteers Everyone who offered to help, organised by role — Choir, Sermonette, Song Leading, Ushers, AV Tech, Setup & Takedown, Children's Program, Hospitality.
📥 Export Download data to Excel/Google Sheets for printing or sharing.

Understanding Registration Statuses

Status What it means
📝 Draft Person has started filling in the form but hasn't submitted it yet. You may want to follow up with them.
✉️ Submitted Person has completed and submitted their registration — it's waiting for your review.
✅ Confirmed You've confirmed their spot.
❌ Cancelled Registration has been withdrawn.

If the numbers look out of date, click the small ↻ Refresh link in the top-right corner of the page.

5. Downloading Data (Export Tab)

The Export tab lets you download the registration data into a spreadsheet.

1
Click the 📥 Export tab.
2
Choose which file you want:
  • Master Attendance List — all households with contacts, dates, and family size
  • Volunteer Roster (Full) — all volunteer preferences in detail
  • Choir Roster by Voice Part — sorted Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass
3
Click the yellow ⬇️ Download CSV button. The file will save to your Downloads folder automatically.
4
Open the downloaded file with Microsoft Excel (or Google Sheets if you prefer).
🔒 Privacy reminder
These files contain personal information about your congregation members. Please store them securely, do not forward them externally, and delete them when Feast coordination is finished — as required under the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

6. Signing Out

When you're finished, click the yellow "Sign Out" button in the top-right corner of any page.

7. If Something Goes Wrong

Problem What to try
Can't log in Double-check your email and password. Use "Forgot password?" to reset if needed.
Page shows a red error message Try refreshing the page (press F5, or the ↺ button in your browser). If it continues, contact the web developer.
Numbers seem too low Some households may still be in "Draft" — they started but haven't submitted yet. Check the Registrations tab filtered by "Draft" to see who to follow up with.
Taken to "My Registration" instead of Admin Make sure you signed in with jon.pinelli@cogwa.org. Sign out and sign back in with the correct email.
Downloaded file looks garbled in Excel Open Excel, go to File → Open → Browse, select the file, and in the import wizard choose UTF-8 encoding.

8. Adding Another Admin

If you'd like someone else to have admin access (e.g. a deacon or deaconess helping with coordination), contact the web developer and provide their email address. Admin access is not something members can grant themselves — it requires a code change, which keeps everyone else's data safe.
